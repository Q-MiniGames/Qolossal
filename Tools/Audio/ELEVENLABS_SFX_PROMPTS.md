# Qolossal sound effects: ElevenLabs prompts

ElevenLabs Sound Effects makes **one sound per prompt**, so this sheet is a list of prompts, one for each effect.
For each effect, paste the **style line** first and then the effect's prompt.
- Set the duration to the value given; "auto" is fine for anything under 2 s.
- Turn **Loop** on for the effects marked `loop`.
- Generate 3–4 takes of each and keep the best. Make variations for sounds that repeat a lot, such as footsteps and hits.

Save each file as `SFX_<Group>_<Name>_<nn>.wav` (for example `SFX_Qori_Step_Moss_01.wav`). Then send me the folder and I'll import and connect them.

## Style line (paste before every prompt)

> Hand-crafted 2D fantasy platformer sound effect, warm, organic and soft-edged, natural materials (wood, leaf, moss, limestone, sap, water), gentle and storybook-like rather than harsh or sci-fi, clean dry recording with no music and no voice, short natural tail.

**Mystery rule:** none of these should sound like a creature or a body. Quakes and the swell are **earth and stone**. At most they can have a faint low warmth that sounds like a slow pulse.
