## Native resolution — not production accepted

The generator returned **2172 × 724**, despite the larger requested canvas. Native and imported sizes are identical; no upscaling or source resizing is performed. Import: sRGB, uncompressed, no NPOT rescale, Clamp, bilinear, mipmaps, max4096. At the 48 × 16 placement this is 45.25 source pixels/world unit. The closest 4K camera requires 216 pixels/world unit, so a production painting at this exact placement requires **10368 × 3456** new native detail, or equivalently registered native panels. A 3840 × 2160 render of this trial is a composition capture, not proof of native 4K artwork. `resolution.json` explicitly fails the native-4K gate. Do not ship or mark this draft accepted on the strength of the seam tests.
