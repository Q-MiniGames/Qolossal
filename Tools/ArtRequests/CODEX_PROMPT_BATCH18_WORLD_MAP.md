Subject: a colossal titan lying on its back in a wide valley, seen exactly side-on. Feet at the left, toes pointing up. One knee raised high. The thigh slopes down into a deep sheltered lap. The belly and chest rise gently to the shoulder, a dip at the neck, then the head at the upper right, propped against the valley wall, face turned up, eye closed. Its near hand rests on the ground beside the lap.

The body is landscape, in the approved Chalk Orchard look: limestone caps, root-tendon seams, olive meadow moss, small trees. Pale nail-like cliffs at the toe tips. A long old road up the shin. Farm terraces and one mill on the knee. A small lantern-lit town (Lantern Vaults style: homes tucked under curved overhangs of warm rock) in the lap. A forest growing between tall parallel stone walls across the chest. Bare windy ridges over the shoulder. On the face: water falling over the chin, pale ridged steps at the mouth, a long bare ridge for the nose, a lake with a seam across it over the closed eye, reeds along its edge, a pale dome above.

Around it: floating slabs of rock with moss on top, in four loose clusters: above the shin, over the lap and town, over the chest, and a rising stair toward the face. A few dark cave mouths in the body's slopes, and one large arch under the raised knee.

Light: warm dawn sky (match `BG_Sky_Terraces`). Readable as a whole body at this distance: this is the spoiler view of the whole world.

No text, no labels, no route lines, no UI, no frame.
